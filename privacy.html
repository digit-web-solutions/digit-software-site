Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Digit ("Digit," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with this website, digit.software (the "Website"). This policy applies only to the Website and does not extend to any other Digit product, service, or business practice not described here.

Information We May Receive

The Website is a static informational site. We do not operate user accounts, checkout systems, or customer databases through the Website, and we do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information through it.

If you contact us directly (for example, by emailing info@digit.software), we receive whatever information you choose to include in that message, such as your email address and the contents of your communication. We use that information only to respond to you.

Website and Technical Data

Like most websites, ordinary technical information may be processed automatically as part of serving web pages -- for example, IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, and the pages requested. This Website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a third-party hosting service; GitHub processes standard web request data as necessary to deliver the Website, subject to GitHub's own privacy practices.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Website does not set its own cookies and does not use tracking pixels, analytics scripts, or advertising technologies. The underlying hosting infrastructure may use technical mechanisms necessary to serve the site reliably; we do not control those independently of GitHub Pages' own infrastructure.

How Information Is Used

Any information we receive directly from you (such as through an email you send us) is used solely to respond to your inquiry and for legitimate related business purposes, such as recordkeeping.

Third-Party Services

The Website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, or embedded widgets on the Website. If that changes in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not share information you send us with third parties except where necessary to operate the Website (such as our hosting provider processing standard technical request data) or where required by law.

Data Retention

We retain direct correspondence (such as emails sent to info@digit.software) for as long as reasonably necessary to address the inquiry and for legitimate recordkeeping purposes.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information we handle, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

External Links

The Website may reference or link to third-party resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of any third-party site.

Children's Privacy

The Website is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to info@digit.software.

About Digit

An AI-native operator, not a legacy agency

Digit is directed by Justin Goncalves and built around AI-assisted engineering: software agents handle much of the day-to-day building, testing, and operating work, under explicit human review and approval. Digit is early-stage and growing deliberately — we would rather be accurate about where we are today than overstate it.

Digit's earlier incarnation, in 2024, operated as a web development agency. Digit today is a different kind of company: an operator of its own digital businesses, built on internal automation and applied AI rather than client services.

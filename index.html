Build. Operate. Learn.

The cycle does not end at launch. Real-world operation creates the feedback that informs better software, stronger infrastructure, and the next generation of systems.

Governed Autonomy

Autonomy with accountability.

Digit builds systems capable of carrying increasingly complex work from intelligence to execution. Autonomy operates within defined authority, clear operational boundaries, and explicit governance for consequential actions.

Routine work can move quickly through automated systems. Actions involving meaningful commitments, external publication, spending, or other consequential decisions remain subject to appropriate authorization.

Capability is not authority.

Capability

The system has the technical ability to act.

Authority

The action falls within its permitted scope.

Execution

The system proceeds when capability and authority align.

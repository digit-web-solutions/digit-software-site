Control Architecture

Autonomy, engineered with boundaries.

Greater autonomy requires better control.

Digit designs systems with explicit boundaries between technical capability, operational authority, and execution. Software can move quickly within defined scope while consequential actions remain subject to the controls appropriate to them.

This makes autonomy more useful, more predictable, and more capable of operating in real environments.

Autonomy is not the absence of control. It is the product of well-engineered control.

Capability

The system has the technical ability to act.

Authority

The action falls within its permitted scope.

Execution

The system proceeds when capability and authority align.

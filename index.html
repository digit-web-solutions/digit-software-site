Operating Model

An operating loop, not a one-off automation

Every business Digit runs moves through the same loop — understanding what's true, deciding what to do, and learning from what happens.

01

Understand

What's actually true right now.

02

Decide

What should happen next, and why.

03

Coordinate

Route work to the right place.

04

Execute

Act through controlled connections.

05

Verify

Confirm what actually happened.

06

Learn

Turn results into knowledge.

Autonomy without control is not an operating model. Routine work can move through this loop with real independence. Anything consequential still requires a human decision — see below.

Human Control

Autonomy, with accountability

Software can increasingly do the work. It should not increasingly decide, on its own, what's worth doing. Digit's systems are built to know the difference between being capable of an action and being authorized to take it.

Routine operations move fast, inside clear limits. Anything consequential — spending, publishing, external commitments — routes to an explicit human decision, every time. That boundary doesn't loosen as the systems improve; it's the point of building them this way.

Capable

The system has a technical path to act.

Authorized

A human has explicitly allowed this action.

Only then: act

Capability alone is never treated as permission.

About Digit

Building the system by operating through it

Digit began in 2024 as a web development agency. Today it's a different kind of company: an operator of its own digital businesses, built on applied AI and internal automation rather than client services.

Digit is directed by Justin Goncalves. Software agents handle much of the day-to-day building, testing, and operating work, under explicit human review — Digit's own businesses are the proving ground for the operating model itself.

Digit is early-stage and growing deliberately. We'd rather be accurate about where we are today than overstate it.
